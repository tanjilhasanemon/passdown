# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **PassDown** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy academic and open-source community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward fellow students and contributors
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints, learning levels, and academic backgrounds
* Sharing only legitimate, accurate, and safe educational resource links
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback during code and content reviews
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Sharing malicious, unsafe, or copyright-infringing links
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in an academic or professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and the deployed PassDown application) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer (**Tanjil Hasan Emon**) via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/tanjilhasanemon/passdown/issues). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
